How to use the hub

  • Tap a tile on the home screen to open it, and the arrow at the top to come back.
  • You only see the sections the owner has switched on for your group.
  • Where a section says you can view but not change it, you can read everything but not add to it.
  • Use People to keep contact details for parents, guardians, family members, and lawyers. Adding someone there does not give them access. The owner invites people from the Access page.
  • Tap + Add on the home screen to jump straight to writing a message, expense, or note.

Storage and backup

Measuring…

This counts messages, posts, photos shared on the Timeline and everything else kept in the database (the free Supabase plan allows about 500 MB). Documents and chat files are stored separately (about 1 GB on the free plan). Photos are shrunk to under 190 KB each.

Alerts and the number on the icon

Checking…

On an iPhone, add the hub to your Home Screen first (Share → Add to Home Screen), open it from there, then tap the button. The red number counts unread messages and notifications. It updates while the app is open or just after you leave it.
