How to use the hub

  • Tap a tile on the home screen to open it, and the arrow at the top to come back.
  • You only see the sections the owner has switched on for your group.
  • Where a section says you can view but not change it, you can read everything but not add to it.
  • Use People to keep contact details for parents, guardians, family members, and lawyers. Adding someone there does not give them access. The owner invites people from the Access page.
  • Tap + Add on the home screen to jump straight to writing a message, expense, or note.
